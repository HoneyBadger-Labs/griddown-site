Why GRIDDOWN Exists

GRIDDOWN is a project of Honey Badger Labs — built on a simple premise: the tools most people depend on for critical knowledge assume a working internet connection. GRIDDOWN doesn't.

Whether you're off-grid by choice, dealing with a natural disaster, or simply done depending on cloud systems you don't control — GRIDDOWN puts a complete, AI-assisted knowledge base in your hands. On a thumb drive. No connection required.

The Project

GRIDDOWN is currently in development. The system is being built around 11 curated knowledge modules — medicine, farming, water, shelter, energy, security, communications, navigation, weapons, mental health, and vehicles — sourced from military field manuals, USDA guides, and domain-vetted references.

Full details, specs, and early access information are available on the main page.
