The GRIDDOWN Product

The GRIDDOWN USB drive operates entirely offline. It collects no data from you, sends nothing over the network, and has no telemetry, analytics, or tracking of any kind. Everything it does happens locally on your machine.

This Website

We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising scripts on getgriddown.com. We do not use cookies for tracking purposes.

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with any other party. Web3Forms' own privacy policy governs their handling of form submissions.
